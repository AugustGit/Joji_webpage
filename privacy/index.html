1. Eligibility

The App is intended for users aged 16 years or older.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will delete it.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address (used for login & account verification)
  • Phone number (used for account verification)
  • Profile photo
  • Name and profile details you choose to provide
  • Content you post (events, comments)
  • Messages sent to other users
  • Connection data (1st, 2nd, 3rd degree connections)
  • If you contact us, we may collect additional information you provide.

B. Public Profile Photo Storage

Your profile photo is stored in a public storage bucket with a randomly generated file name.

While the file name is not identifiable, the image itself may be publicly accessible to anyone with the direct link.

By uploading a profile photo, you acknowledge and accept this risk.

C. Automatically Collected Information

We may collect:

  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Log data
  • Usage data
  • Analytics data (e.g., user engagement, session duration, features used)

We may use third-party analytics providers.

D. Verification Information

To verify your account:

  • We send confirmation emails.
  • We send password reset confirmation codes.
  • We validate accounts using phone numbers.

We do not use phone numbers for marketing.

3. How the App Works (Visibility & Connections)

  • 1st-degree connections can view all your posts by default. If you do not wish a 1st-degree connection to view a post, various options are available but must be actively chosen.
  • 2nd and 3rd-degree connections can view your posts only if you enable this visibility.
  • Users who can view an event post may comment on it.
  • Users may send direct messages to one another.

You are responsible for adjusting your visibility settings.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the App
  • Authenticate users

  • Enable messaging and commenting
  • Send push notifications
  • Improve functionality and analytics
  • Prevent abuse and fraud
  • Enforce our Terms

We do not sell personal data.

We do not currently send marketing emails, but reserve the right to introduce marketing communications in the future (with required consent where legally necessary).

5. Push Notifications

We may retain limited information if required by law or for legitimate business purposes.
